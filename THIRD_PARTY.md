# Third-party dependencies

- Open5GS: https://github.com/open5gs/open5gs — separate upstream license.
- UERANSIM: https://github.com/aligungr/UERANSIM — separate upstream license.
- docker_open5gs: https://github.com/herlesupreeth/docker_open5gs — separate upstream license.

The bootstrap script downloads `docker_open5gs` into `vendor/` only when executed. Review upstream licensing and terms before redistributing third-party source, images or configurations.
